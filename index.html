Motif model. A candidate is a tyrosine in the mature chain that could start a 4–8 residue peptide and has a second aromatic residue (F, Y or W) nearby. Aromatic at position 3–4 is the strong case, matching F4 of met-enkephalin and F3 of β-casomorphin-7; aromatic at 5–6 is the weaker case, which is what recovers YPVEPF and both lactoferroxins. Enk score compares the first five residues to YGGFM, scoring 2 per identity and 1 per conservative match, out of 10.

What counts as evidence. A peptide supports a site only when its N-terminus sits at that tyrosine. Peptides that merely span the tyrosine are reported separately in the project TSVs and do not count here, because a blocked N-terminus abolishes opioid binding.

Coverage. Goat and sheep sequences were scanned but have no peptide data in this set, so they are excluded from these figures. Humans do not express β-lactoglobulin. Sheep lactoferrin is an unreviewed UniProt entry.
